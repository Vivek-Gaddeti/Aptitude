Q7 Pattern: Successive change · back-calculating the original
A jacket's price is raised by 30% and then, in a sale, cut by 30%. It now sells for ₹910. What was the price before the rise?
Not quite. It's C, ₹1,000
Your time 0:52 · target 0:45 · 7 s over
Why missed?

Solution

  1. Net change for +a% then −b%: a − b − ab/100 = 30 − 30 − 9 = −9%
  2. So the final price is 91% of the original: 0.91 × P = 910
  3. P = 910 ÷ 0.91 = ₹1,000
Shortcut
Up x% then down x% is always a loss of x²/100 %. Here 900/100 = 9%, so 910 is 91% → original 1,000.
Trap
Adding 9% back to 910 (option B). The 9% is of the original, so divide by 0.91. Don't multiply by 1.09.
